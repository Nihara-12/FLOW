\# FLOW — Civic Intelligence Platform



> Turning community reports into intelligent action.



\## 🌍 The Problem



Citizens encounter potholes, drainage failures, broken streetlights,

waste accumulation and other civic problems every day.



However, reporting is often fragmented, difficult to track and disconnected

from administrative response.



\## 💡 Our Solution



FLOW creates an intelligent bridge between citizens and civic administrators.



Citizens can:



\- Report real-world civic problems

\- Describe issues naturally

\- Provide location information

\- Receive AI-powered issue analysis

\- Track the progress of their reports



Administrators can:



\- Monitor community reports

\- Search and filter issues

\- View AI-generated severity scores

\- Identify potential safety risks

\- Prioritize responses

\- Track issues from reporting to resolution



\## 🤖 AI-Powered Civic Triage



FLOW uses AI to analyze citizen reports and generate:



\- Issue category

\- Severity score

\- Confidence score

\- Potential hazards

\- Recommended administrative action



This transforms an unstructured complaint into actionable civic intelligence.
